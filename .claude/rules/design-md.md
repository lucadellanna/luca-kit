**DESIGN.md: when and what.** When a Gemini review thread is rejected as a design decision (not a bug), update DESIGN.md in the same commit -- this prevents the thread from being raised again. Inclusion bar: only non-intuitive choices that a future reader would otherwise question or reverse. Default decisions, obvious choices, and user-stated choices do not qualify; a bloated DESIGN.md hides the decisions that actually need defending.
