**`audit-claude`** scans your project's `./CLAUDE.md` and your global `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` for redundant or bloated content, plus content that belongs in memory, a path rule, a skill, or a hook instead. When both files exist, it also flags scope mismatches: project rules that should be global and vice versa. **Example:** "My CLAUDE.md files have grown messy after months of additions" → `/luca-kit:audit-claude`.
